FRANCESCOBENDINI

ABOUT

Francesco Bendini (Sansepolcro AR,1996) is a sculptor and conceptual artist based in Turin, Italy.

The artist's research is based on a sculptural, process-driven and behavioural approach. His works create a conceptual tension where materials and objects raise questions (?), oscillating between solemnity and mocking irony. Themes such as introspection, youthness and the gray shades of contemporaneity emerge through a personal and generational hauntology, with subtle references to the Italian context.
